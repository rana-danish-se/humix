# Writing guidance and tools

Humix's short-comment writing guidance is informed by selected ideas from
[Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) and
[Stop Slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop), reviewed October 13, 2026.
It is adapted for questions, humor, grief and brief conversation. It does not import
their entire skill or follow blanket bans on punctuation, adverbs or question openings.
